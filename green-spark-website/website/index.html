Making an impact

Green Spark works with households, local government and partners to bring clean energy, safe water and cleaner cooking to rural communities, and turns that change into carbon projects that buyers and investors can trust.

Three priority programmes lead our work: clean cooking, rooftop solar MRV and safe drinking water. Our cookstove projects are registered with Gold Standard; the other programmes follow in sequence.

Our approach

A holistic approach, built with communities

Green Spark Solutions Australia takes a holistic approach to its projects.

Community engagement

We work closely with local communities to understand their needs and preferences, so that our solutions are culturally appropriate and sustainable.

Partnerships

We collaborate with government agencies, NGOs and private-sector organisations, drawing on their expertise and resources to maximise our impact.

Research and development

We invest in research and development to keep improving our products and to explore new technologies for emerging challenges.
